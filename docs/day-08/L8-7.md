## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 WoE and IV

    For each bin: WoE = ln(% of goods ÷ % of bads); IV = Σ (%good − %bad) × WoE.

    | Variable | IV | Rule of thumb |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | bureau_score | 1.346 | very strong (bureau scores legitimately; otherwise suspect leakage) |
    | channel | 0.448 | strong |
    | income | 0.203 | medium |
    | inquiries_6m | 0.192 | medium |
    | utilisation | 0.133 | medium |
    | age | 0.083 | weak |
    | dti | 0.043 | weak |
    | tenure | 0.008 | not useful |
    

    Utilisation WoE by band: ≤50% 0.39 · 50–70% 0.02 · 70–80% -0.26 · 80–90% -0.85 · >90% -1.58 — monotone, matching the F04 curve. **Missing income** gets its own bin: bad rate
    16.3% (WoE -1.54) — missingness is informative (F02). Never impute it away.

    ### 2.2 Points

    Logistic regression on WoE, scaled so **600 points = odds 50:1** and **20 points double the odds** (factor 28.85,
    offset 487.1). Bureau score bins earn (0, 600] 56 pts · (600, 650] 72 pts · (650, 700] 101 pts · (700, 750] 131 pts · (750, 1000] 170 pts. Average score 603.
    Gini **0.654**, KS **0.502** — within application-scorecard norms (Gini 0.4–0.7).

    ### 2.3 Expected loss

    **EL = PD × LGD × EAD.** E.g. PD 4%, LGD 60%, EAD 6,000 → EL 144 per account. PD from the scorecard (calibrated, L6.6), LGD from
    recoveries, EAD from limit × credit conversion factor. IFRS 9 / CECL extend this to lifetime losses.

    ### 2.4 Vintage curves — cumulative 90+ DPD rate by months on book

    | Vintage | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | 2023Q1 | 0.0% | 0.82% | 1.52% | 1.98% |
    | 2024Q1 | 0.0% | 0.44% | 1.42% | 2.0% |
    | 2024Q4 | 0.0% | 0.71% | 1.65% | 2.62% |
    | 2025Q2 | 0.0% | 2.44% | 5.28% | 8.03% |
    

    Vintages compare like with like (same age). 2025Q2 runs at roughly **3.7×**
    earlier vintages at month 9 — the partner channel (L5.7). Calendar-time delinquency mixes ages and hides this.

    ### 2.5 Roll rates (last 12 months)

    | From → to | current | 1–29 | 30–59 | 60–89 | 90+ |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | current | 96.3% | 2.5% | 1.2% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
    | 1-29 | 96.4% | 2.5% | 1.1% | 0.0% | 0.0% |
    | 30-59 | 18.7% | 13.4% | 0.0% | 68.0% | 0.0% |
    | 60-89 | 26.5% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 73.5% |
    | 90+ | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 100.0% |
    

    68.0% of 30–59 accounts roll to 60–89 next month (only 18.7% cure); 73.5% of 60–89
    roll to 90+. Collections effort is most valuable at 30–59.

    ### 2.6 Reject inference (F18)

    We only observe performance for approved applicants. Declined applicants (median bureau 596 vs
    695 for booked; 52.4% below 600 vs 4.7%) are missing exactly where
    risk is highest. Booked low-score accounts already default at 18.2%. Models built on booked data alone
    understate risk at the cut-off (collider, L4.4); remedies: small controlled approvals below the cut-off, bureau performance on
    declined applicants, parcelling/augmentation (with care).

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Scorecards are binned, monotone, points-based logistic models — explainable and stable. Vintages and roll rates monitor the
        book. Booked-only data hides the rejects.

    **Judgement points**

    - **IV > 0.5 needs a story** — bureau score yes; anything else, suspect leakage.
    - **Vintage curves are the early-warning system** — 2025Q2 is already at 2–3× prior vintages; act on channel and cut-off.
    - **Expected loss drives pricing and provisions** — PD calibration matters (L6.4/L6.6).

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"IV table, monotone WoE, points, Gini/KS out of time — and how the latest vintages compare at the same age."*

??? example "F8-10 · Applied · Points and performance"

??? example "F8-11 · Basic · Vintage curves"

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** 2023Q1: 1.52% at 9m; 2024Q1: 1.42% at 9m; 2024Q4: 1.65% at 9m; 2025Q2: 5.28% at 9m;  *(tolerance: ± 0.2 pp)*

??? example "F8-12 · Applied · Roll rates"
    **Business framing:** Collections planning.

    **Deliverable:** 12-month roll-rate matrix; 30→60, 60→90, cure from 30.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** 30→60 68.0%; 60→90 73.5%; cure 18.7%. *(tolerance: ± 1 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Intervene at 30–59; most roll forward.

        **Common wrong answer:** Averaging buckets.

    **Leader drill:** Where would extra agents go?

??? example "F8-13 · Applied · Expected loss"
    **Business framing:** Provisioning estimate.

    **Deliverable:** EL per account and portfolio with PD 4%, LGD 60%, EAD 60% of limit.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** EL = PD × LGD × EAD; compute with the average limit. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Calibrated PDs required (L6.6).

        **Common wrong answer:** Using uncalibrated scores as PDs.

    **Leader drill:** How would the 2025 vintages change EL?

??? example "F8-14 · Stretch · Reject inference"
    **Business framing:** Can we lower the cut-off?

    **Deliverable:** Compare declined vs booked bureau scores; booked low-score default.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Declined median 596 vs 695; 52.4% vs 4.7% below 600; booked low-score default 18.2%. *(tolerance: ± 1 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Risk below the cut-off is unobserved; run a controlled test.

        **Common wrong answer:** Extrapolating the booked model.

    **Leader drill:** Design the below-cut-off test.

??? example "F8-15 · Stretch · Churn model"
    **Business framing:** Retention wants a churn model.

    **Deliverable:** Outline features, target window, censoring handling and evaluation.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Features at observation point; target churn in next 6 months among active; survival or discrete-time models; PR-AUC and lift. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Churn = survival problem with censoring (L9.2).

        **Common wrong answer:** Training on churned vs never-churned regardless of observation time.

    **Leader drill:** What retention action would the model trigger?
