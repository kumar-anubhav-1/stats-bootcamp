## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 Measures (rule A → B)

    | Measure | Definition | Meaning |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Support | P(A and B) | how common the pair is |
    | Confidence | P(B \| A) | how often B follows A |
    | Lift | P(B \| A) ÷ P(B) | > 1: together more than chance |

    **Apriori** speeds the search: an item set can only be frequent if all its subsets are frequent.

    ### 2.2 Rules in 3,163 baskets (subcategory level, support ≥ 1%: 1,192 rules)

    | Rule | Support | Confidence | Lift |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Pasta → Pasta Sauce | 13.3% | 80.5% | 3.28 |
    | Pasta Sauce → Pasta | 13.3% | 54.3% | 3.28 |
    | Beer → Chips | 5.4% | 44.7% | 2.72 |
    | Cleaning → Paper | 3.3% | 34.2% | 1.95 |
    | Paper → Cleaning | 3.3% | 19.0% | 1.95 |
    

    The planted associations are recovered: Pasta ↔ Pasta Sauce (lift 3.278) and Beer ↔ Chips (lift 2.716),
    R21. Lift is symmetric; confidence is not (80% of pasta baskets have sauce; 54% of sauce baskets have pasta).

    ### 2.3 The trivial-rule trap

    Fruit appears in 38.5% of all baskets, so almost anything "predicts" it:
    **Pasta → Fruit** has confidence 63.4% but lift only
    1.64. Rank by **lift with a support floor**, not by confidence.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Lift finds genuine affinity; support says whether it matters; confidence alone flatters popular items. Associations suggest
        actions to test, not proven effects.

    **Judgement points**

    - **Test before reorganising stores.** Placing sauce next to pasta may cannibalise impulse browsing; run a store test (Day 4).
    - **Bundles need margin maths** — discounting items people already buy together gives away margin.
    - **Finance cross-sell:** product-holding affinities are confounded by life stage; use uplift models (L9.1) to target.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"Lift and support for each rule, and what test would prove the action works?"*

## 5. Output anatomy — `rcp_basket`

| Column | Read as |
| --- | --- |
| support | share of baskets with both |
| confidence | P(consequent \| antecedent) |
| lift | affinity vs chance |
| trivial flag | consequent is a very common item |

## 6. Scenario matrix

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | Goal | Filter |
    | --- | --- |
    | strong affinities | lift ≥ 1.5, support ≥ 1% |
    | recommendations | confidence + lift per antecedent |
    | avoid noise | support floor; ignore tiny counts |

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"

        **Common wrong answer:** Acting on confidence.

    **Leader drill:** Rewrite the slide headline.
