**Objectives** — you can: build a graph of entities linked by shared attributes and find connected components (rings); detect
threshold bunching (structuring); combine both with model scores; and set escalation thresholds with compliance in mind.

## 1. Business question

=== "Retail"

    > "Are coupon abusers coordinating across accounts?"

=== "Finance"

## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 Rings (F07)

    Accounts are linked when they share a device or address. Genuine sharing is small (households: 2–3 accounts — the largest ordinary
    component here has 2). Connected components with ≥ 6 accounts: **3**, sizes
    [15, 15, 15]; fraud rate inside them 0.6, 0.6, 0.6 vs a base rate of
    0.7%. Network features are the strongest fraud signal we have (L6.5).

    ### 2.2 Structuring (F08)

    Cash deposits of 10,000 or more trigger a report. Monthly maximum cash deposits by 1,000 bands:

    | Band | 5k | 6k | 7k | 8k | 9k | 10k | 11k | 12k | 13k | 14k | 
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
    | Count | 1,574 | 1,047 | 739 | 482 | 1,606 | 277 | 202 | 137 | 132 | 79 | 

    The distribution falls smoothly — except for a spike in **9,000–9,999** (1,606) and a cliff above 10,000
    (277): ratio **5.8**. **100** accounts deposited just under the
    threshold in 6 or more months — the planted structurers.

    ### 2.3 Combining signals

    Score = model probability (L6.5) + ring membership + structuring pattern + velocity. Prioritise cases where independent signals
    agree; review whole rings at once.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Rings show up as unusually large connected components; structuring as bunching just below a threshold. Both are cheap,
        explainable detectors that complement models.

    **Judgement points**

    - **Escalation is regulated:** suspicious-activity reports (SARs) follow AML procedure — analytics supplies evidence, compliance
      decides; avoid tipping off.
    - **Graph data is sensitive** (devices, addresses): purpose limitation and access control apply.
    - **Thresholds move behaviour:** once structurers learn a pattern is watched, they adapt — monitor near-threshold bands too.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"Which independent signals agree for this case, and what does compliance need to file?"*

### Finance · Meridian

??? example "F8-5 · Basic · Fraud rings"
    **Business framing:** Find rings.

    **Deliverable:** Run `finance_fraud_rings.yaml`.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** 3 components ≥ 6, sizes [15, 15, 15], fraud 60% each vs 0.7%. *(tolerance: exact)*

        **Model answer:** Large shared-link components are rings.

        **Common wrong answer:** Flagging households of 2–3.

    **Leader drill:** Review process for a ring?

??? example "F8-6 · Applied · Structuring"
    **Business framing:** AML asks for evidence of structuring.

    **Deliverable:** Run `finance_structuring.yaml`; accounts below 10,000 in 6+ months.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Ratio 5.8 (1,606 vs 277); 100 accounts. *(tolerance: ± 1%)*

        **Model answer:** Spike below the threshold; refer accounts to compliance.

        **Common wrong answer:** Contacting customers (tipping off).

    **Leader drill:** What goes in the referral?

??? example "F8-7 · Stretch · Combine signals"
    **Business framing:** Prioritise fraud reviews.

    **Deliverable:** Propose a combined priority score (model, ring, structuring, velocity).

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Weighted or rule-based; prioritise cases with independent agreeing signals. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Independent evidence raises precision.

        **Common wrong answer:** Single-signal queues.

    **Leader drill:** How would you validate the weights?
