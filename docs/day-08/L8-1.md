## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 Methods

    | Method | Finds | Needs | Watch |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | K-Means | round, similar-size groups | k, scaled features | sensitive to scale and outliers |
    | Hierarchical | a tree of merges (dendrogram) | linkage choice | slow for large n |
    | DBSCAN | dense regions; labels noise | density parameters | struggles with varying density |
    | Gaussian mixture | soft (probabilistic) clusters, elliptical shapes | k, covariance type | can overfit |

    ### 2.2 Customer segments (893 customers with ≥ 2 baskets, 18 features)

    Features: baskets, revenue (log), average basket, promo share, price ratio, list price, category shares — **all scaled**.

    | k | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
    | Silhouette | 0.158 | 0.163 | 0.159 | 0.085 | 0.087 | 0.092 | 0.085 | 

    Silhouette (−1 to 1; > 0.5 strong, < 0.25 weak) peaks at k = 3 but is **weak everywhere** — customers form
    a continuum, not tidy islands. The planted truth has 5 segments; K-Means with k = 5 recovers them only partly (adjusted Rand index
    **0.336**, R23).

    | Cluster (k = 5) | Customers | Median baskets | Median revenue | Promo share | Top category |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | 2 | 191 | 4.0 | 264 | 13.0% | Fresh Produce |
    | 4 | 121 | 2.0 | 136 | 11.0% | Fresh Produce |
    | 1 | 259 | 2.0 | 104 | 11.6% | Fresh Produce |
    | 3 | 185 | 2.0 | 72 | 7.8% | Beverages |
    | 0 | 137 | 3.0 | 71 | 64.4% | Snacks |
    

    Even with weak overall structure, one segment is unmistakable: **promo hunters** (promo share ~64%, Snacks-heavy) — distinct,
    sizeable and actionable (targeted offers, margin control).

    **Scaling matters:** clustering the same features unscaled agrees with the scaled solution only ARI 0.127 —
    revenue's large numbers dominate.

    <div class="sb-chart" data-chart="kmeans3d" data-caption="Rotate and drag k: clusters overlap (low silhouette) — real customers rarely form tidy blobs."></div>

    ### 2.3 PCA

    Principal components are uncorrelated combinations of features ordered by variance (L2.7). The first two explain
    **31.4%**; 10 are needed for 80% — information is spread out. Use PCA for visualisation and
    de-noising; t-SNE/UMAP make pretty maps but distort distances — don't cluster on them.

    <div class="sb-chart" data-chart="pca3d" data-caption="Rotate: 14 features squeezed into 3 components; colour = promo share."></div>

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Clustering always returns clusters. The questions are whether they are distinct, stable and useful. Scale features; choose k
        with silhouette plus business sense; profile and name segments by what you would do differently.

    **Judgement points**

    - **Actionability beats silhouette.** A weak-structure solution with one clearly actionable segment (promo hunters) is worth more
      than a "perfect" k nobody can use.
    - **Stability check:** re-run with different seeds and periods; segments that reshuffle are not a basis for strategy.
    - **Personas need numbers** — size, value, behaviour — not just names.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"How big is each segment, how would we treat it differently, and does it survive a re-run next quarter?"*

## 6. Scenario matrix

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | Data | Method |
    | --- | --- |

    | need soft membership | Gaussian mixture |
    | noise/outliers, arbitrary shapes | DBSCAN |

    | many correlated features | PCA first |

??? example "R8-1 · Basic · Customer segments"
    **Business framing:** Marketing wants customer segments.

    **Deliverable:** Run `retail_cluster_customers.yaml`; silhouette by k; k = 5 profiles.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"

    **Leader drill:** Which segment would you act on first?

??? example "R8-2 · Applied · Scaling matters"
    **Business framing:** A junior clustered unscaled features.

    **Deliverable:** ARI between scaled and unscaled k = 5 solutions.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** ARI 0.127. *(tolerance: ± 0.05)*

        **Model answer:** Unscaled, revenue dominates distances; scale first.

        **Common wrong answer:** Clustering raw revenue with shares.

    **Leader drill:** What review step would catch this?

??? example "R8-3 · Stretch · Recovering planted segments"
    **Business framing:** How well does K-Means find the true segments?

    **Deliverable:** ARI vs planted segments; PCA variance of 2 components; components for 80%.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** ARI 0.336; PCA2 31.4%; 10 components for 80%. *(tolerance: ± 0.05)*

        **Model answer:** Segments overlap in behaviour; clustering recovers them only partly.

        **Common wrong answer:** Treating cluster labels as ground truth.

    **Leader drill:** How stable must segments be before CRM uses them?
