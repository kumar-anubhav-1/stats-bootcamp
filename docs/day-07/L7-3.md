## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 Rolling-origin backtest

    Re-fit at several origins and forecast h periods ahead each time — exactly as the model would be used.

    | Origin (train through week) | Seasonal naive WAPE | Regression WAPE |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | 78 | 10.3% | 6.6% |
    | 86 | 3.4% | 4.6% |
    | 94 | 7.0% | 5.0% |
    | **mean** | **6.9%** | **5.4%** |

    The regression wins on average but not at every origin (origin 86: seasonal naive is better). One window can tell either story.

    <div class="sb-chart" data-chart="forecast" data-caption="Switch methods: every forecast is judged on weeks it never saw, against seasonal naive (MASE < 1 beats it)."></div>

    ### 2.2 Metrics

    | Metric | Formula idea | Strength | Trap |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | MAE / RMSE | average absolute / squared error | units, RMSE punishes big misses | scale-dependent |
    | MAPE | mean of \|error\| ÷ actual | intuitive % | explodes near zero; favours under-forecasts |
    | sMAPE | symmetric % | bounded | still odd near zero |
    | **WAPE** | Σ\|error\| ÷ Σ actual | robust to zeros; volume-weighted | hides small items |
    | **MASE** | MAE ÷ in-sample naive MAE | comparable across series; < 1 beats naive | MAE-based (rewards zeros for sparse items) |
    | **Bias** | Σ(forecast − actual) ÷ Σ actual | direction of error | can hide large offsetting errors |

    ### 2.3 Accuracy depends on the level

    Same approach, Beverages, weeks 79–104: chain WAPE **6.5%**, store level (chain forecast split by stores'
    historic shares) **12.8%**. Aggregates are smoother. Report accuracy at the level decisions are made — store
    replenishment needs store-level accuracy, not the chain figure.

    ### 2.4 Norms (rule of thumb, retail)

    Weekly chain-category WAPE 5–10%; store-SKU-week WAPE 30–60%; intermittent items much worse. Finance: balance forecasts at
    portfolio level typically within a few per cent over a quarter; bias is usually the bigger concern than WAPE.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Judge forecasts by rolling backtests, with WAPE/MASE for accuracy and bias for direction, at the level decisions are made.

    **Judgement points**

    - **MAPE in board packs is misleading** for low-volume items and rewards under-forecasting.
    - **Track bias monthly** — persistent bias is a process problem (planner overrides, stale drivers) more than a model problem.
    - **Accuracy targets must name the level** (store-SKU-week vs chain-category-month) — the numbers differ several-fold.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"What level, what horizon, how many origins — and what's the bias?"*

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | asymmetric costs | bias + cost-weighted error |

## 8. Exercises

### Retail · NorthStar

??? example "R7-6 · Basic · Rolling-origin backtest"
    **Business framing:** Is the regression reliably better?

    **Deliverable:** WAPE of seasonal naive and regression at origins 78, 86, 94 (horizon 8).

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** origin 78: 10.3% vs 6.6%; origin 86: 3.4% vs 4.6%; origin 94: 7.0% vs 5.0%; means 6.9% vs 5.4%. *(tolerance: ± 0.3 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Better on average, not at every origin.

        **Common wrong answer:** Choosing a model from one window.

    **Leader drill:** How many origins would you require?

??? example "R7-7 · Applied · Accuracy by level"
    **Business framing:** Store replenishment wants an accuracy KPI.

    **Deliverable:** Chain WAPE vs store-level WAPE (chain forecast split by historic shares).

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Chain 6.5%, store 12.8%. *(tolerance: ± 0.5 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Aggregates are smoother; report accuracy at the decision level.

        **Common wrong answer:** Quoting chain accuracy for store decisions.

    **Leader drill:** Rewrite the KPI definition.

??? example "R7-8 · Stretch · Metric choice"
    **Business framing:** A dashboard reports MAPE for all SKUs.

    **Deliverable:** Explain why MAPE fails for intermittent SKUs and propose replacements.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** MAPE is undefined/infinite at zero actuals and rewards under-forecasting; use WAPE (portfolio), MASE (per item), bias. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Metrics must suit zeros and the asymmetric cost of errors.

        **Common wrong answer:** Dropping zero weeks from the metric.

    **Leader drill:** Which metric goes in the supply-chain scorecard?

### Finance · Meridian

??? example "F7-6 · Basic · Bias first"
    **Business framing:** Finance cares about bias.

    **Deliverable:** Explain why bias matters more than WAPE for provisioning forecasts.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Systematic under-forecasting of losses under-provisions; offsetting errors in WAPE matter less. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Track bias by month and segment.

        **Common wrong answer:** Reporting only WAPE.

    **Leader drill:** What bias tolerance would you set?

??? example "F7-7 · Applied · Backtest design for monthly data"
    **Business framing:** Only 42 months of history.

    **Deliverable:** Design a rolling-origin backtest (origins, horizon).

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** e.g. origins at months 30, 33, 36, 39 with 3-month horizons; accept fewer origins and wider uncertainty. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Short histories limit validation; be explicit about it.

        **Common wrong answer:** Random k-fold on months.

    **Leader drill:** How do you communicate the uncertainty?

??? example "F7-8 · Stretch · Level matters"
    **Business framing:** Branch forecasts look poor.

    **Deliverable:** Explain why branch-level accuracy is lower than portfolio accuracy.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Smaller numbers, more noise; aggregate errors cancel. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Set targets at the decision level.

        **Common wrong answer:** Comparing branch WAPE with portfolio WAPE.

    **Leader drill:** Which level should staffing use?
