## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 The model families

    | Family | Idea | Seasonality | Drivers |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Naive / seasonal naive | next = last value / same period last year | seasonal naive: yes | no |
    | ETS (Holt, Holt-Winters) | weighted average of the past, updated level/trend/season | Holt-Winters: needs ≥ 2 full cycles to fit | no |
    | ARIMA / SARIMA | regression on own past values and past errors | SARIMA: seasonal lags | ARIMAX/SARIMAX: yes |
    | Prophet-style regression | trend + Fourier seasonality + holiday dummies + drivers | Fourier terms | yes |

    ### 2.2 Beverages: train weeks 1–78, test weeks 79–104 (mean 5,720 units/week)

    | Model | WAPE | MASE | Bias |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | naive | 13.3% | 0.84 | -12.8% |
    | seasonal_naive | 6.9% | 0.43 | 1.5% |
    | moving_avg_8 | 10.0% | 0.63 | -8.8% |
    | holt_damped | 9.6% | 0.6 | -8.1% |
    | regression_fourier | 6.5% | 0.41 | 1.3% |
    | sarimax | 6.6% | 0.41 | 1.2% |
    

    - **Seasonal naive** (copy last year's week) halves the naive error — seasonality is most of the forecast.
    - **Damped Holt** (no seasonality, since two full years aren't available for training) under-forecasts the summer — bias
      -8.1%.
    - **Regression with Fourier terms + holiday + temperature** and the equivalent **SARIMAX** win: WAPE
      6.5% with small bias. They know about holidays and weather — but in production the temperature
      input must itself be a *forecast*.

    ### 2.3 Finance balances

    Six-month hold-out of total balances: damped Holt WAPE **1.2%** vs naive 36.8%
    (naive bias 36.8%). The portfolio is growing; a model with trend matters here, seasonality doesn't.

    ### 2.4 Over-engineering

    Each model choice adds parameters to tune and monitor. A model is worth keeping only if it beats seasonal naive **consistently
    across several forecast origins** (L7.3) by enough to change decisions.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Baselines first (seasonal naive). Add structure the business understands — holidays, promotions, weather — through
        regression terms. Complexity must win across many backtests, not one chart.

    **Judgement points**

    - **"Our ML forecast" without a seasonal-naive comparison is unfinished work.**
    - **Drivers must be available at forecast time**: planned promotions yes; actual temperature no (use the weather forecast).
    - **Bias costs money asymmetrically**: under-forecast → stock-outs and lost sales; over-forecast → waste (perishables) or working
      capital.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"How much better than seasonal naive, across how many origins, with what bias?"*

## 8. Exercises

### Retail · NorthStar

??? example "R7-3 · Basic · Baselines first"
    **Business framing:** Forecast Beverages for weeks 79–104.

    **Deliverable:** Naive, seasonal naive and 8-week moving average: WAPE and bias.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Naive 13.3% (bias -12.8%); seasonal naive 6.9% (1.5%); moving average 10.0%. *(tolerance: ± 0.3 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Seasonal naive halves the naive error: seasonality is most of the signal.

        **Common wrong answer:** Skipping baselines.

    **Leader drill:** What improvement over seasonal naive would justify a new model?

??? example "R7-4 · Applied · Regression with seasonality and drivers"
    **Business framing:** Add Fourier seasonality, holiday and temperature.

    **Deliverable:** Log-linear regression and SARIMAX(1,0,0) with the same regressors; WAPE, MASE, bias.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Regression 6.5% (MASE 0.41); SARIMAX 6.6%. *(tolerance: ± 0.3 pp)*

        **Model answer:** Known drivers beat pure time-series structure; temperature must be a forecast in production.

        **Common wrong answer:** Using actual temperature in a live forecast.

    **Leader drill:** Which drivers do you have reliable forecasts for?

??? example "R7-5 · Stretch · Why damped Holt is biased"
    **Business framing:** Damped Holt under-forecasts the test period.

    **Deliverable:** Explain its bias and when Holt-Winters would be appropriate.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Bias -8.1%: no seasonal component (Holt-Winters needs two full cycles to fit; only 78 weeks available). *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Model structure must match the data's patterns and history length.

        **Common wrong answer:** Blaming the parameters.

    **Leader drill:** How much history do you need for annual seasonality?

### Finance · Meridian

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"

??? example "F7-4 · Applied · Drivers known in advance"

    **Deliverable:** List drivers available at forecast time vs not.

        **Model answer:** Only use drivers known (or forecast) at forecast time.

        **Common wrong answer:** Using next month's actual payments.

    **Leader drill:** Who supplies the planned drivers?
