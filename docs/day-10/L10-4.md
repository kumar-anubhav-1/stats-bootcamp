## 2. Part B · data exam tasks

Your instructor gives you the held-out data folder. Point your configs at it by changing **only** `dataset.path`.

=== "Retail (seed 7001)"

    1. Data quality: count duplicates, negative quantities and 100× price errors; state your treatment.
    2. One store's sales shifted during the year: find the store and week, and estimate the size of the shift.
    3. A category experiment ran in test vs control stores: estimate the lift with a CI (DiD).
    4. Find the store with fabricated-looking numbers (Benford/last digit, compared with peers).
    5. Find the week the online discount field changed and explain it.
    6. Estimate cannibalisation of the promoted pack on its sibling.
    7. Identify the stores where return abuse concentrates.

=== "Finance (seed 7002)"

    1. Data quality: impossible ages, negative incomes, informative missing income.
    2. Fraud rings: number and size of rings; fraud rate inside them.
    3. Structuring: how many accounts deposit just under the threshold repeatedly?
    4. Champion/challenger: cure-rate difference with CI; check SRM and guardrails.
    5. Fairness: approval-rate ratio by group, and the proxy.
    6. Find the branch whose amounts fail Benford.
    7. Default rate and drift of recent vintages vs early ones.

Each task: the number, its uncertainty, the method (Navigator node), the config you ran, one sentence of interpretation.

## 3. Part C · code-review exam (Leader)

You receive a junior analyst's notebook and slide claiming *"Loyalty members are 35% more valuable and the summer promotion added 18%
to sales; recommend doubling both."* Find what's wrong (there are 7 flaws, covering data quality, method, inference and interpretation),
say how each changes the conclusion, and make the decision: approve, revise or reject — with what you'd need to see.

## 5. Exercises (exam preparation)

### Retail · NorthStar

??? example "R10-12 · Basic · Exam prep: quality"
    **Business framing:** Held-out data, same schema.

    **Deliverable:** List the quality checks you'll run first and the configs.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** rcp_quality: duplicates (line_id), negative quantities, price ratio > 50, missingness, reconciliation; rcp_profile. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Same configs, new path.

        **Common wrong answer:** Assuming the teaching anomalies repeat exactly.

    **Leader drill:** What differs in a held-out seed?

??? example "R10-13 · Applied · Exam prep: shift and experiment"
    **Business framing:** Find a store shift and an experiment lift.

    **Deliverable:** Which recipes and settings?

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** rcp_changepoint (share_of_total) per store; DiD/growth-ratio for the test category with pre-period. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Reuse L7.5 and L9.1 configs, change unit/category.

        **Common wrong answer:** Post-only comparison.

    **Leader drill:** How would you report uncertainty?

??? example "R10-14 · Applied · Exam prep: fabricated store"
    **Business framing:** Benford on held-out data.

    **Deliverable:** Approach and threshold.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** rcp_benford by store vs peer distribution of MAD; last-digit test; refer, don't accuse. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Peer comparison beats absolute thresholds.

        **Common wrong answer:** Flagging any MAD > 0.012 without peers.

    **Leader drill:** Escalation path?

??? example "R10-15 · Stretch · Code review"
    **Business framing:** 'Loyalty members 35% more valuable; summer promo +18%.'

    **Deliverable:** List likely flaws before seeing the notebook.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Selection bias (R20), bulk buyers and price errors in revenue, no control for promo, post-only comparison, duplicates, multiple testing, no CIs. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Checklist from Days 1–9.

        **Common wrong answer:** Approving because numbers look plausible.

    **Leader drill:** Approve, revise or reject?

### Finance · Meridian

??? example "F10-12 · Basic · Exam prep: rings and structuring"
    **Business framing:** Held-out finance data.

    **Deliverable:** Which configs, and what will differ?

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** finance_fraud_rings / finance_structuring with the new path; ring count/size and structurer count differ by seed. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Reuse; report what you find, not what you expect.

        **Common wrong answer:** Writing '3 rings' from memory.

    **Leader drill:** How do you check a colleague didn't copy teaching answers?

??? example "F10-13 · Applied · Exam prep: champion/challenger"
    **Business framing:** Held-out test.

    **Deliverable:** Steps before quoting the lift.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** SRM, balance (SMD), primary metric with CI, guardrails (complaints, recoveries), decision rule. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** L4 checklist.

        **Common wrong answer:** Lift only.

    **Leader drill:** Decision if CI crosses zero?

??? example "F10-14 · Applied · Exam prep: Benford branch"
    **Business framing:** One branch's amounts are suspicious.

    **Deliverable:** Approach.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** rcp_benford by branch, compare MAD with peers, last-digit test, refer to audit. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** Peer comparison.

        **Common wrong answer:** Testing amounts that don't span orders of magnitude.

    **Leader drill:** Audit referral content?

??? example "F10-15 · Stretch · Code review (finance)"
    **Business framing:** 'New score AUC 0.98 — deploy now.'

    **Deliverable:** Likely flaws and decision.

    ??? success "Expected finding and model answer"
        **Expected:** Leakage (recovery/collections fields), random split instead of out-of-time, no calibration, reject inference, fairness unchecked → reject until fixed. *(tolerance: —)*

        **Model answer:** L6.4 + L9.5 + L9.6 checklist.

        **Common wrong answer:** Deploying on AUC.

    **Leader drill:** What evidence would make you approve?
