# Statistics Navigator

**Start from the business question. Answer a few questions about your data. Get the technique, what to check, and what to do if
a check fails.** Every lesson starts with a "You are here" marker that links to its node in this map.

!!! info "Rules of thumb"
    Thresholds shown in the Navigator (VIF > 5–10, PSI bands, AUC ranges, n ≥ 30) are **rules of thumb**. They vary by company,
    product and regulator. Use them to start a conversation, not to end one.
