# L11.2 · Image classification, augmentation and transfer learning

<p class="lesson-meta">Day 11 · Optional module · Basic → Advanced 75 min · Leader 30 min · Prerequisites: L6.4–L6.6, L11.1 · Recipe: prompt-driven (scikit-learn; optional PyTorch)</p>

{{ here("N-ML-DL") }}

**Objectives** — you can: frame a vision problem as classification ("is this slot empty?"); compare a simple rule, hand-made
features, raw pixels and a small neural network on a fair split; explain why accuracy misleads when gaps are rare; use **data
augmentation** to make a model robust to lighting; and explain **transfer learning** — reusing a model pretrained on millions of
photos.

!!! info "In plain words"
    We cut every shelf photo into narrow windows and ask a model one yes/no question per window: *empty slot or not?* Then we test it
    on stores and conditions it has never seen — because that is where vision models fail.

## 1. Business question

=== "Retail"

    > *"Train it on six stores, roll it out to all of them. Will it still work in store S07, whose camera went dim, or in S03, whose
    > camera is out of focus?"*

=== "Finance"

    > *"Before reading the amount, sort each scanned image: cheque, ID card or utility bill. Same idea — one label per image."*

## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 The set-up

    Each photo is cut into 16-pixel-wide windows every 4 pixels. A window is labelled **gap** if a person's box covers ≥ 75% of it,
    **product** if it covers none, and left out if in between. Training: stores S01, S02, S04, S05, S06, S08
    ({{ a.v_n_train_windows | n }} windows, only {{ a.v_gap_share }}% gaps). Testing: unseen stores S09 and S10
    ({{ a.v_n_test_windows | n }} windows), plus the dim camera (S07, weeks 5–8) and the blurry one (S03).

    ### 2.2 Four approaches, four failure modes

    | Model | Accuracy (new stores) | Gaps found (recall) | Flags that are real gaps (precision) | Accuracy, dim camera | Gaps found, dim | Gaps found, blurry |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    {% for k, v in a.v_cls.items() %}| {{ k }} | {{ v.acc_test }}% | {{ v.rec_test }}% | {{ v.prec_test }}% | {{ v.acc_dim }}% | {{ v.rec_dim }}% | {{ v.rec_blur }}% |
    {% endfor %}

    Read it row by row:

    - **Brightness rule** — dark packs look like gaps: only {{ a.v_cls["Brightness rule (dark window = gap)"].prec_test }}% of its
      flags are real, and in the dim store almost everything looks dark.
    - **Hand-made features** — excellent in normal light, but in the dim store the colours shift and accuracy falls to
      {{ a.v_cls["Logistic regression on hand-made features"].acc_dim }}% (a flood of false alarms).
    - **Raw pixels + logistic regression** — copes with dim light but finds only
      {{ a.v_cls["Logistic regression on raw pixels"].rec_blur }}% of gaps in blurry photos.
    - **Small neural network** — near perfect on new stores, yet in the dim store it finds only
      {{ a.v_cls["Small neural network on raw pixels"].rec_dim }}% of gaps while still scoring
      {{ a.v_cls["Small neural network on raw pixels"].acc_dim }}% accuracy.

    ### 2.3 The accuracy trap

    Gaps are rare, so a model that says "no gap" everywhere is right most of the time. That is why the table reports **recall**
    (share of real gaps found) and **precision** (share of flags that are real) — exactly the lesson of L6.5, now on images.

    ### 2.4 Data augmentation

    Make extra training images by changing what *shouldn't* matter — here, brightness (random 50–110%). The model learns that a gap
    is a gap in any light. Retrained with augmentation, the same network finds **{{ a.v_aug.rec_dim }}%** of gaps on the dim camera
    (was {{ a.v_cls["Small neural network on raw pixels"].rec_dim }}%) and {{ a.v_aug.rec_blur }}% on the blurry one, without losing
    anything on new stores ({{ a.v_aug.rec_test }}% recall, {{ a.v_aug.prec_test }}% precision).

    Other common augmentations: small shifts and rotations, flips (if the meaning doesn't change), crops, blur, colour jitter, noise.

    ### 2.5 Transfer learning

    Real shelf photos are bigger and messier than ours. Instead of training from scratch, start from a CNN **pretrained** on millions
    of everyday photos (e.g. ResNet, EfficientNet, a vision transformer). Its early filters already detect edges, textures and shapes;
    you replace the last layer and **fine-tune** on a few thousand labelled shelf images. This is how most industrial vision
    systems are built, and it needs far less data and compute.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Vision models fail on conditions they didn't see in training. Test on new stores and bad cameras, report recall and
        precision, augment for what shouldn't matter, and start from pretrained models.

    **Judgement points**

    - **"99% accurate" is meaningless for rare events** — ask for gap recall and precision by store and camera.
    - **Insist on a hold-out of whole stores** (not random windows), or performance will look better than reality.
    - **Augmentation and transfer learning are cheap insurance**; ask whether they were used.
    - **Label quality caps model quality** (L11.5).

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"How does it do on the worst camera, not the average one?"*

## 5. Output anatomy

| Item | Read it as |
| --- | --- |
| accuracy | misleading when the event is rare |
| recall (gaps found) | missed gaps = lost sales nobody sees |
| precision (true flags) | false flags = wasted staff walks |
| per-camera results | where the model will fail in rollout |

## 6. Scenario matrix

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | great on test, poor in one store | different light/camera | augmentation, more varied training data |
    | high accuracy, low recall | rare class | report recall/precision, adjust threshold |
    | fails on blurry images | quality | fix camera; blur augmentation |
    | little labelled data | — | transfer learning |

=== "Leader"

    | Claim | Ask | Norm |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | "98% accuracy" | recall/precision per store and camera | rare-event metrics |
    | "ready for all stores" | hold-out stores, worst-camera results | test where it will run |

## 7. Planted traps and reuse drill

!!! warning "Traps"
    The accuracy trap; V1 dim camera; V2 blurry camera; random-window splits leaking the same photo into train and test.

**Reuse drill:** add blur augmentation as well as brightness — does gap recall on S03 hold up for every model?
