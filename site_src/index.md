## How a lesson works

Every topic starts from a **real industry problem** at NorthStar (grocery retail) or Meridian (retail bank) — the kind solved in
analytics teams worldwide — never from a textbook definition. Then:

1. **📍 Where you are** on the mental map, and **today's mental map** at the top of each day.
2. **Business question** in retail and banking versions.
3. **Concept** in two layers — *Basic → Advanced* and *Leader* — with **interactive charts** built from the real data: drag
   sliders to change a parameter, rotate 3-D charts, switch models, and watch the result change.
4. **Prompt cards** for Claude Code, then an optional **🛠 learn-by-doing lab**: you direct Claude Code through the industry task,
   check the result, change one thing and explain it back.
5. **Output anatomy** (what every number means) and a **scenario matrix** (situation → action → industry norm).
6. **Planted trap and reuse drill**, **exercises** on both datasets, and a **quiz** where every option explains why it is right or
   wrong — so the quiz teaches as you answer.
