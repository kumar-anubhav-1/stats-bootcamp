## 1. The two briefs (pick one; Leaders review both)

=== "Retail"

    > **"Did the Q4 promotion plan work?"** — Trading Director, NorthStar

    The plan for 4 November–31 December 2025 aimed to cut discount spend while protecting sales. Compare with the same weeks of
    2024, decide whether the plan worked, and recommend next year's plan.

=== "Finance"

    > **"Why did default rise in the latest vintage — and what do we do?"** — Chief Risk Officer, Meridian

    The 2025Q3 vintage is defaulting far faster than 2025Q1. Explain why, how much is mix vs deterioration, and what to change.

## 3. Deliverables

1. A one-page readout (pyramid, L9.7) with the decision you want.
2. An appendix: methods, data checks, every number with its source file and config.
3. Committed configs that reproduce your numbers.
4. Five minutes presenting + ten minutes of challenge.

## 4. Challenge panel (Leaders sit on it)

| Panel question | What a strong answer includes |
| --- | --- |
| "How much of this is real vs mix/seasonality?" | decomposition or control group with numbers |
| "How sure are you?" | CIs, sensitivity, what would change your mind |
| "What exactly should we do Monday?" | action, owner, metric, test |
| "What did you check in the data?" | quality checks, anomalies found |
| "Who could this harm?" | fairness / customer impact (L9.5) |

## 5. Rubric

Same five criteria as the transfer test (correctness, assumptions, interpretation, action, clarity & reuse), 1–5 each; pass ≥ 3 on all
and ≥ 18 in total.
