## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 The model families

    | Family | Idea | Seasonality | Drivers |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Naive / seasonal naive | next = last value / same period last year | seasonal naive: yes | no |
    | ETS (Holt, Holt-Winters) | weighted average of the past, updated level/trend/season | Holt-Winters: needs ≥ 2 full cycles to fit | no |
    | ARIMA / SARIMA | regression on own past values and past errors | SARIMA: seasonal lags | ARIMAX/SARIMAX: yes |
    | Prophet-style regression | trend + Fourier seasonality + holiday dummies + drivers | Fourier terms | yes |

    ### 2.2 Beverages: train weeks 1–78, test weeks 79–104 (mean {{ a.d7_bev_mean | n }} units/week)

    | Model | WAPE | MASE | Bias |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    {% for k, v in a.d7_fc.items() %}| {{ k }} | {{ v.wape }}% | {{ v.mase }} | {{ v.bias }}% |
    {% endfor %}

    - **Seasonal naive** (copy last year's week) halves the naive error — seasonality is most of the forecast.
    - **Damped Holt** (no seasonality, since two full years aren't available for training) under-forecasts the summer — bias
      {{ a.d7_fc.holt_damped.bias }}%.
    - **Regression with Fourier terms + holiday + temperature** and the equivalent **SARIMAX** win: WAPE
      {{ a.d7_fc.regression_fourier.wape }}% with small bias. They know about holidays and weather — but in production the temperature
      input must itself be a *forecast*.

    ### 2.3 Finance balances

    Six-month hold-out of total balances: damped Holt WAPE **{{ a.d7_bal_wape_holt }}%** vs naive {{ a.d7_bal_wape_naive }}%
    (naive bias {{ a.d7_bal_bias_naive }}%). The portfolio is growing; a model with trend matters here, seasonality doesn't.

    ### 2.4 Over-engineering

    Each model choice adds parameters to tune and monitor. A model is worth keeping only if it beats seasonal naive **consistently
    across several forecast origins** (L7.3) by enough to change decisions.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Baselines first (seasonal naive). Add structure the business understands — holidays, promotions, weather — through
        regression terms. Complexity must win across many backtests, not one chart.

    **Judgement points**

    - **"Our ML forecast" without a seasonal-naive comparison is unfinished work.**
    - **Drivers must be available at forecast time**: planned promotions yes; actual temperature no (use the weather forecast).
    - **Bias costs money asymmetrically**: under-forecast → stock-outs and lost sales; over-forecast → waste (perishables) or working
      capital.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"How much better than seasonal naive, across how many origins, with what bias?"*

## 6. Scenario matrix

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | Series | Start with |
    | --- | --- |
    | strong annual seasonality, ≥ 1 year | seasonal naive baseline |
    | known drivers (holidays, promos, price) | regression / SARIMAX with regressors |
    | trend, no seasonality | damped Holt |
    | ≥ 2–3 full seasonal cycles | Holt-Winters / SARIMA |
    | many sparse items | intermittent methods (L7.4) |
