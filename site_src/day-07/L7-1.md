## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 The components

    **Series = trend + seasonality + remainder** (additive) or **× each other** (multiplicative — seasonal swings grow with the level,
    as in retail; log the series to make it additive). STL (Seasonal-Trend decomposition using Loess) estimates the parts robustly.

    Chain weekly units ({{ a.d7_weeks }} weeks, mean {{ a.d7_mean_units | n }}):

    | Pattern | Measured | Planted (R11) |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Seasonal strength (STL) | {{ a.d7_seas_strength }} (0–1) | annual cycle |
    | Trend strength | {{ a.d7_trend_strength }} | — |
    | Fri–Sat vs Mon–Thu baskets | ×{{ a.d7_frisat_ratio }} | +30% |
    | Q4 (non-holiday) vs other weeks | ×{{ a.d7_q4_ratio }} | +25% |
    | Holiday weeks vs others | ×{{ a.d7_hol_ratio }} | +40% |

    The peak week (week {{ a.d7_peak_week }}, {{ a.d7_peak_units | n }} units) is a holiday week. With only two years of data, the
    seasonal component is estimated from two cycles — enough to see it, not enough to model every holiday precisely.

    ### 2.2 Stationarity

    A **stationary** series has constant mean and variance over time — the assumption behind ARIMA models.

    | Series | ADF p (H0: non-stationary) | KPSS p (H0: stationary) |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Chain weekly units | {{ a.d7_adf_p }} | {{ a.d7_kpss_p }} |
    | Monthly delinquency rate | {{ a.d7_dq_adf_p }} | — |

    Chain units fluctuate around a stable level with seasonality (both tests agree it is stationary around its seasonal pattern). The
    delinquency rate climbs from {{ a.d7_dq_first }}% to {{ a.d7_dq_last }}% over {{ a.d7_dq_months }} months — **not stationary**.
    Differencing (month-to-month changes) is the ARIMA remedy, but the real story is a **regime shift**: a single change-point splits
    it at {{ a.d7_dq_break_month }} ({{ a.d7_dq_before }}% → {{ a.d7_dq_after }}%), the arrival of the partner vintages seasoning on
    book (L5.7, L7.5).

    ### 2.3 ACF and PACF

    The **autocorrelation function** correlates the series with its own past: lag-1 ACF {{ a.d7_acf1 }} (this week resembles last
    week) and **lag-52 ACF {{ a.d7_acf52 }}** (this week resembles the same week last year — annual seasonality). The PACF shows the
    direct effect of each lag after shorter lags are accounted for; it guides AR orders in ARIMA (a sharp PACF cut-off after lag p →
    AR(p)).

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Decompose before modelling: trend, seasonality, holidays, remainder. Non-stationarity often signals a change in the business
        (mix, policy) rather than a statistical nuisance.

    **Judgement points**

    - **Year-on-year comparisons must align calendars** (holiday weeks, Fri–Sat counts, Q4) — otherwise "growth" is just the calendar.
    - **Rising delinquency is a population story** until proven otherwise: check vintage and channel mix before blaming the economy.
    - **Two years is a short history** for annual seasonality — expect uncertainty around holiday effects.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"Show me the decomposition and the calendar effects before any forecast."*
