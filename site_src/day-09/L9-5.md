## 2. Concept

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    ### 2.1 Disparate impact (F15)

    | Group | Approval rate |
    | --- | --- |
    | A | {{ a.d9_appr_A }}% |
    | B | {{ a.d9_appr_B }}% |

    Disparate impact = {{ a.d9_appr_B }} ÷ {{ a.d9_appr_A }} = **{{ a.d9_di }}** — below the 0.8 rule of thumb.

    ### 2.2 The proxy

    Approval by region: {{ a.d9_appr_by_region | kv('%') }}. Region R4 is **{{ a.d9_r4_share_B }}%** group B.

    | | Group A | Group B | Ratio |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Outside R4 | {{ a.d9_appr_nonR4.A }}% | {{ a.d9_appr_nonR4.B }}% | {{ a.d9_di_nonR4 }} |
    | In R4 | {{ a.d9_appr_R4.A }}% | {{ a.d9_appr_R4.B }}% | |

    Most of the gap runs through region: dropping the protected attribute does not remove it when a proxy carries the information.

    ### 2.3 Error-rate fairness

    Model on booked accounts, protected attribute excluded, declining the riskiest 15%:

    | Group | Flagged | Actual default | Mean PD | TPR (defaulters flagged) | FPR (good customers flagged) |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    {% for g, v in a.d9_fair.items() %}| {{ g }} | {{ v.flag_rate }}% | {{ v.default_rate }}% | {{ v.mean_pd }}% | {{ v.tpr }}% | {{ v.fpr }}% |
    {% endfor %}

    Default rates are similar, yet group B is flagged more and good group-B customers are wrongly flagged more often
    (FPR {{ a.d9_fair.B.fpr }}% vs {{ a.d9_fair.A.fpr }}%) — the R4 indicator pushes PDs up for B. **Definitions conflict:** when base
    rates differ, no model can equalise calibration, TPR and FPR at once; choose the criterion with legal and business owners.

    ### 2.4 Conformal prediction: honest uncertainty

    Split conformal builds prediction sets with a guaranteed coverage. At 90%:

    | Method | Coverage overall | Defaulters covered | Non-defaulters covered | Ambiguous sets (both outcomes) |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Marginal | {{ a.d9_conf_coverage }}% | {{ a.d9_conf_cov_def }}% | {{ a.d9_conf_cov_nondef }}% | {{ a.d9_conf_both_share }}% |
    | Class-conditional (Mondrian) | — | {{ a.d9_confm_cov_def }}% | {{ a.d9_confm_cov_nondef }}% | {{ a.d9_confm_both_share }}% |

    Marginal coverage hits 90% by always predicting "no default" — it covers no defaulters. Coverage guarantees are **on average**;
    ask for them **per group/class** that matters (here, defaulters; in fairness, protected groups). The "both" sets flag the cases
    the model genuinely can't call — route them to manual review.

=== "Leader"

    !!! abstract "Refresh"
        Leaving out protected attributes is not fairness. Measure outcomes and errors by group, look for proxies, and decide which
        criterion you're held to.

    **Judgement points**

    - **Regulatory exposure:** a 0.74 disparate impact driven by geography resembles redlining — investigate whether R4's lower
      approval is justified by risk, and document it.
    - **Model risk management:** inventory, independent validation, performance and fairness monitoring, change control, and an owner.
    - **Responsible AI** extends to personalised pricing: test prices by neighbourhood and protected-group proxies before launch.

    !!! tip "Coaching card"
        Ask: *"Which fairness metric are we committing to, and what does it cost in accuracy or approvals?"*

## 5. Output anatomy

| Item | Read it as |
| --- | --- |
| disparate impact | ratio of favourable-outcome rates; < 0.8 = flag |
| TPR/FPR by group | equal opportunity / error parity |
| calibration by group | same PD → same default rate |
| conformal coverage | guaranteed on average over the calibration population |

## 6. Scenario matrix

=== "Basic → Advanced"

    | Concern | Metric |
    | --- | --- |
    | outcome gaps | disparate impact / demographic parity |
    | missed opportunities | equal opportunity (TPR) |
    | wrongful adverse action | FPR parity |
    | same score, same meaning | calibration within groups |
    | hidden proxies | feature–group association; model with and without the proxy |

=== "Leader"

    | Finding | Response |
    | --- | --- |
    | DI < 0.8 | investigate justification; consider less discriminatory alternatives |
    | proxy found | assess business necessity; remove or constrain |
    | uneven errors | adjust thresholds or features with legal sign-off |
